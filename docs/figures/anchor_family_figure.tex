  \caption{\textbf{Anchor Family.} All variants operate on one shared library of
  history-derived candidates. NaiveAnchor, ExpoAnchor, and SparseAnchor use
  fixed or history-structure rules; ERMAnchor chooses the candidate and scalar
  coefficient minimizing pooled train--validation risk; AutoAnchor applies an
  ordered structural prior and falls back to ERM when no prior fires. The
  selected specification $\theta_v=(a_v,\beta_v)$ is frozen per variable before
  test evaluation. At inference, the selected candidate is evaluated at the
  requested query times, postprocessed with $\beta_v$ and recent-history
  clipping, and returned as the forecast. Dots denote observed values and open
  dots denote predictions; the plotted trajectories are illustrative.}
