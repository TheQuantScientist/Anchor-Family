    \caption{\textbf{The Anchor family.}
    \textbf{(a)} AutoAnchor calibrates one candidate $a_v$ and scale coefficient
    $\beta_v$ per variable using train and validation data. ERMAnchor minimizes
    pooled masked MSE in benchmark scale over the shared library, comparing
    identity and fitted scaling with the postprocessing in (b).
    An ordered prior uses observed histories, query times, and window metadata;
    a matching rule replaces the ERM choice and sets $\beta_v=1$.
    Otherwise, the ERM choice is retained.
    \textbf{(b)} The frozen candidate is evaluated on each new history at the
    requested times, multiplied by $\beta_v$, inverse-transformed, and clipped
    using recent observed history.
    \textbf{(c)} All five variants share this forecast pipeline and differ in
    their choice of $a_v$ and $\beta_v$. Miniature curves are illustrative.}
